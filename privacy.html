PRIVACY BY DEFAULT

Privacy

Version 3 is designed to collect less data, not more.

What stays on your device

When Private Rescue mode is on, the free-text problem description you type is not written into LONA’s persistent browser history. The app may store minimal on-device statistics such as case ID, rescue category, safety level, device type and outcome so your dashboard can work.

What LONA does not ask for

  • Passwords or passphrases
  • PINs or mobile-money PINs
  • One-time verification codes
  • Card numbers or banking credentials
  • Recovery codes

Sharing

A Rescue Report or feedback leaves your device only when you deliberately use a copy, download or share action. Version 3 contains no advertising trackers or third-party analytics code.

Your control

You can use “Clear My Local Data” on the Rescue dashboard to remove the minimal LONA statistics and accessibility preferences stored in that browser.

Browser storage is convenient, not a secure vault. Do not type secrets into the Rescue Assistant.
