SAFETY FIRST

Safety & Limits

LONA ICT Rescue is designed for safe first-line checks. Some situations should not be handled as ordinary DIY troubleshooting.

Stop and get qualified help when you notice

  • A swollen battery
  • Smoke, sparks, fire or burning smell
  • Liquid inside or on a powered device
  • Repeated severe overheating or sudden shutdowns
  • Important data on a failing or clicking storage device
  • Suspected ransomware, serious account takeover or financial fraud

Security rule

LONA will never tell you to send a password, PIN, one-time code, card number or mobile-money PIN to receive help.

Limits

Device models and operating systems differ. When instructions conflict with the device manufacturer’s official guidance, use the manufacturer’s current official guidance or a qualified technician.
