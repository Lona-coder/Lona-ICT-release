ABOUT LONA

About LONA ICT Rescue

LONA ICT Rescue is an independent digital first-aid service from LONA ICT Hub. It helps ordinary users work through common technology problems in clear language, with safety limits built into the rescue path.

What Version 3 is: a guided troubleshooting engine with an evidence-informed rescue library. It is not presented as artificial intelligence and it does not pretend to diagnose every technical fault.

What makes LONA different

  • Human-friendly problem descriptions instead of technical jargon.
  • Clear low-risk, caution and stop-and-get-help decisions.
  • Private-by-default design with no advertising trackers or third-party analytics in Version 3.
  • Rescue Reports users can keep or share when seeking further help.
  • Offline access after installation on supported browsers.

Ownership

The LONA ICT Rescue name, interface copy, original brand assets and product experience are presented as LONA ICT Hub materials. Third-party product names are used only to identify the platforms users may need help with.
