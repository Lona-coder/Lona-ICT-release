STOP SIGNALS

Don’t keep troubleshooting if you notice:


  • Swollen battery
  • Smoke, sparks or burning smell
  • Liquid inside a powered device
  • Repeated sudden shutdown from severe heat
  • Important data at risk of being overwritten

SMART HABITS
Small habits. Fewer ICT emergencies.




🔐
Protect accounts
Use unique passwords and turn on multi-factor authentication wherever it is available.


⬆️
Keep software updated
Updates often fix security and reliability problems. Use official update channels.


💾
Back up what matters
Keep an extra copy of important documents, photos and school/work files before trouble starts.


🎣
Pause on urgent messages
Scammers rely on panic. Verify using a contact method or website you already know is genuine.


🔑
Prefer passkeys where offered
Passkeys are phishing-resistant and can be easier to use than passwords. Keep a secure recovery method too.


🧑‍💻
Be careful with remote access
Do not install remote-control software because an unexpected caller or message tells you to.
